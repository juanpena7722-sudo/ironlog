- **Status:** Verified, PR #15 awaiting owner approval

- **Date:** 2026-11-11

## Summary

Settings imports a backup with `POST /backup/import` in **merge** (default) or **replace** mode;
replace asks for confirmation first. After sign-in, `LocalDataBanner` offers a one-time import of
the data the browser-only version left in `localStorage['ironlog:data']`, in its stored unit; on
success the key is removed, and a dismissal is remembered. API exports now carry
`canonicalUnit: 'kg'`, so an export from an lb account is not converted a second time, while an
old unmarked lb export is still converted from lb.

**Real-stack check.** Headless Chrome drove the built frontend through the production compose
stack (gateway, API, PostgreSQL). All 57 of 57 checks passed:

- **Local data import.** An old-format `ironlog:data` in lb (sets 225 and 135 lb, weigh-ins 100
  and 200 lb, chest/waist/arms/thighs 40/32/15/24 in, goals unit `lb`) showed the banner.
  **Import local data** stored 102.06 and 61.24 kg, 45.36 and 90.72 kg, and 101.6/81.28/38.1/60.96
  cm; foods and health were unchanged and the account goals were kept. The key was removed, no
  dismissal was stored, and the banner did not return after a reload.
- **Dismissal.** **Dismiss** stored `ironlog:local-import-dismissed`, kept `ironlog:data`, sent
  no import, and the banner stayed hidden after a reload.
- **API backup in an lb account.** The export had `canonicalUnit: 'kg'` with `goals.unit: 'lb'`
  and kg values. Re-importing it with merge (same account, no duplicates; and a fresh lb
  account) and with replace (after the confirmation; **Cancel** sent nothing) kept every value
  in kg with no second conversion, and kept the target weight.
- **Old unmarked lb export.** Imported through Settings into a kg account, by merge and by
  replace, it was converted from lb (replace also stored the 180 lb target as 81.65 kg).
- **Clear all logs and demo data** behave as in TF04: clear empties every collection and keeps
  the goals; demo data fills every collection with body weights in kg.

## Notes

- `POST /backup/import` does not reject `canonicalUnit: 'kg'` together with `unitHint=lb`; the
  frontend never sends that combination. Accepted for this task.
- Backups exported from `main` between the TF04 and TF05 merges have no `canonicalUnit`; that
  build was never released to users.
- The dismissal is stored per browser, not per account.
- `/api/v1/auth/me` shares the auth rate limit of 10 requests per minute per IP. Every import
  invalidates all queries, including the session, so many imports and reloads within a minute
  show `Could not check your session.` with **Retry** until the window resets. This is the
  TF03/TB03 behaviour and was not changed; the real-stack check paces its requests.
