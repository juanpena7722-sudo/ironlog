# TF06 — UX states, accessibility, performance

- **Status:** Verified, PR #16 awaiting owner approval
- **Branch:** feat/tf06-ux-a11y
- **Date:** 2026-11-11

## Summary

Every page shows loading, error with retry, and empty states through `QueryState`, and every
save or delete reports its result through `MutationFeedback` or the Settings messages. Icon-only
controls and unlabelled inputs get accessible names, focus is visible, danger colours meet WCAG
AA, and the layout has no horizontal scroll from 360 px. The Dashboard chart is lazy-loaded, so
Recharts is not in the first load. `GET /auth/me` and `POST /auth/logout` now use only the global
rate limit, and an import no longer fetches the session again, so quick reloads no longer show
`Could not check your session.`

**Real-stack check.** Headless Chrome drove the built frontend through the production compose
stack (gateway, API, PostgreSQL), with an empty account and then with demo data:

- **First-load JS**, gzipped, every script requested on a cold load: `/login` 122,321 B
  (entry, `Auth`, `ui`); logged-in Dashboard 124,193 B (entry, `Dashboard`, `ui`, `utils`,
  `units`). With weigh-ins, the lazy chart adds `WeightTrendChart`, `Area` and
  `generateCategoricalChart` after the data loads: 236,233 B in total.
- **Layout:** no horizontal scroll (`scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth`) at 360, 768 and 1280 px
  on Dashboard, Workouts (including the open log form), Diet, Progress, Health and Settings, and
  at 360 px on `/login` and `/register`.
- **axe-core:** no violations on any of the eight pages.
- **Navigation:** `aria-label` on the six nav links (`Dashboard` … `Settings`) and `Log out`.
- **Session check:** 15 full reloads of the Dashboard in 18 seconds all loaded; none showed
  `Could not check your session.`
- **Login limit:** 10 wrong logins returned 401 and the 11th returned 429 Problem Details with
  `Retry-After: 60`.

## Notes

- Cycle 1 found two failures on the real stack, fixed in `b8b41b6`: the `.sr-only` `Actions`
  headers widened Progress and Health to about 450 px at 360 px (fixed with `.table-wrap`
  `position: relative`), and axe reported `link-in-text-block` (serious) on the `/login` and
  `/register` switch link (fixed by underlining `p a`).
- jsdom cannot measure layout, so the 360 px check and the axe scan are manual until TF07 adds
  them to the Playwright suite.
- The Dashboard total after the chart loads (236.2 KB) is close to the 250 KB budget, but it is
  not part of the first paint.
